Who owns the films?

You do. Your film stays yours and your credits stay yours. We hold a permanent non-exclusive right to screen and promote it, which is how it ends up at our screenings and on our channel.

What happens to it afterwards

It gets screened at a Nobody Club night in front of strangers, which is a completely different experience from showing it to your friends. Festival calendars and what actually fits your film come through the club, free.

The next one could be yours

It will be rough. So was everyone's. The point is that it exists, you finished it, and you now know exactly what you would fix. That last part is the whole education.
