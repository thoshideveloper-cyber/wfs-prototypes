Questions people actually ask

If yours is not here, message us. We reply within a day, and a real person writes the reply.

I have never made anything. Is this for me?

Yes, and that is the whole point. The day is built for people with zero experience. If you have made films before you will still learn things, but you are not the person we designed it for.

Do I need a camera or any equipment?

No. Everything at the weekend and month levels is shot on phones. If someone in your crew owns a tripod or a clip mic they can bring it, but nothing is required.

Do I need to bring a team?

No. Most people come alone and we place them in a crew of five. If you want to bring your own team, you can do that too.

Where do actors come from?

The Nobody Club directory, which is free for actors to list on. You shortlist and we help you make the calls on the day. Crew members are also allowed to act, and often do.

Who teaches?

Working professionals. Assistant directors, ad film directors, editors and theatre directors who do this for a living and teach on weekends. Two mentors per day, moving between the crews.

What if my film is bad?

It will be. So was everyone's, including the founder's, which is currently sitting in a folder marked do not open.

The point is that it exists, you finished it, and you now know exactly what you would fix. That last part is the whole education.

Do I have to travel around the city to shoot?

No, and this is deliberate. Everything is shot within 200 metres of the venue. The cafe, the terrace, the stairwell, the street outside. It sounds limiting until you realise it is the only reason your film gets finished by six.

Is lunch included?

No. We give you tea and snacks, and lunch is at the cafe on your own bill. Their food is better than anything we would arrange, and it is why they give us the space.

Can I do the AI track without technical skills?

Yes. The AI day teaches the tools from zero. What matters more is story sense, and that is taught too.

Do you get my film into festivals?

We do not submit on your behalf and we do not promise selections. What we do is share the festival calendar through The Nobody Club, tell you which ones actually fit your film, and help you get your submission materials in order. The films themselves are yours to send.

Is there a refund?

Full refund up to seven days before the day. After that we have already paid the venue and the mentors, so we can move you to the next batch instead.

How many people are in the room?

Thirty at most, in six crews of five. Two mentors move between them all day. Any bigger and the 6:15 export stops being possible.

Still deciding?

Come to a Nobody Club screening first. Free, most weekends, and you will meet people who were exactly where you are one month ago and are now insufferable about it.
