Bringing your own team?

You can arrive with your own crew of five and make your own film, or come alone and we will place you. Most people come alone. Actors can be cast free from The Nobody Club directory.

Refunds

Full refund up to seven days before the day. After that we have already paid the venue and the mentors, so we move you to the next batch instead.

What is included

The room, two mentors, the five online lessons, tea and snacks, the screening, and your film exported and sent to you. Lunch is at the cafe on your own bill — their food is better than anything we would arrange, and it is why they give us the space.

What to bring

A charged phone, a charger, and a power bank if you own one. For the AI Sunday, a laptop. That is the whole list.

Private days

Corporate film days
