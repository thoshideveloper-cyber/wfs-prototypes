How booking works

Four steps, about two minutes

Pay for the seat

Card, UPI or netbanking through Razorpay. Seats are held only once payment lands, and they do go.

You get the five lessons

Story, frame, sound, performance, cut. Forty minutes each, included in the price. Watch them before the day so the classroom time goes to the parts you cannot learn alone.

Three days before, you get the group

A WhatsApp group for your batch, with the venue pin, the timings and who else is coming.

You show up and make a film

Ten in the morning to seven at night. Crews from a hat, script by half twelve, export at 6:15, everybody watches everything at 6:30.

Bring

Almost nothing

  • A charged phone, and its charger. A power bank if you own one.
  • A laptop, only if this is the AI Sunday.
  • Lunch money. Lunch is at the cafe, on your own bill.
  • No camera, no experience, no contacts, no team. Those are all our problem.
